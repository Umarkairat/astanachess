Astana Open Festival

The main international chess festival of the capital of Kazakhstan.

The international festival “Astana Open” is the calling card of our club and a grand event in the country’s sports calendar. Every year hundreds of chess players, international masters and grandmasters from near and far abroad come to Astana to compete for the title of the strongest.

We create uncompromising and most comfortable conditions for playing. Participants have access to a special debriefing room to analyze played games with our experts, and all played games are immediately entered into the database.

Game groups and cups

The festival brings together tournaments of different age and rating categories.

Children's Cup of the Republic of Kazakhstan

The official stage of the Children's Cup of the Republic of Kazakhstan, which is divided into two main categories:

  • Open U10:Boys and girls under 10 years old.
  • Open U14:Boys and girls under 14 years old.

Adult tournaments

The main competitions for professionals and experienced dischargers:

  • Astana Open A:The official stage of the RK Cup among men and women. The strongest masters and grandmasters participate.
  • Astana Open B:Amateur rating tournament for players with FIDE rating.

Worldwide broadcasts

The first board games of the Open A main tournament are broadcast in real time on the world's leading chess platforms (Lichess, Chess.com, Chess24). This allows chess fans around the world to watch the progress of the battle, and athletes to receive international recognition.

The festival's prize fund is constantly growing: for example, in 2025 it amounted to a record 8.5 million tenge, attracting the elite of the CIS chess world.
