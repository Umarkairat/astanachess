Results of the Championship of the Republic of Kazakhstan 2026

April 2026

At the 2026 Republic of Kazakhstan Chess Championship, our chess school created a real sensation! Our students demonstrated an outstanding level of play and won a total of 27 medals. It is noteworthy that most of them - 14 medals - are of the highest gold standard. There are also 6 silver and 7 bronze awards.

Result: 14 gold, 6 silver, 7 bronze medals Official tables

International tournament "MTS Alliance"

October 2025

CSHC "ASTANA" successfully organized and held the first international tournament under the auspices of the sponsorship of Alliance MTS. The competition brought together the strongest boys and girls not only from all regions of Kazakhstan, but also from the countries of Central Asia. The tournament was held according to the Swiss system and allowed many participants to fulfill the standards of candidates for master of sports.
