How we teach chess

A unique multi-level system for training athletes by category and skill level.

01

Entry level (Amateurs)

Beginning students are taught by patient and experienced trainers (candidate masters of sports). The main goal at this stage is to instill a sincere love for chess, explain the basic rules, the value of pieces and the basics of checkmate.

02

Sports categories (4 and 3 categories)

In-depth study of tactical techniques (double strike, link, enticement), development of spatial imagination. Students begin to regularly participate in club qualifying tournaments.

03

Advanced level (2nd category and above)

Masters of sports work with the guys. The main emphasis is on strategic planning, position assessment, in-depth calculation of variations, study of typical endgames and the formation of a personal opening repertoire.

04

Master group (CCM, Masters, Grandmasters)

The strongest guys are trained by international masters and grandmasters. Individual analysis of games, psychological preparation for the Asian and World Championships and international Olympiads.

Areas of study

We offer flexible class formats to suit everyone's schedule and age.

Offline classes

Classes in spacious modern classrooms on the left and right banks of Astana. Live communication with a coach, playing on professional electronic boards and participation in internal club tournaments.

Online classes

Distance training on interactive platforms from anywhere in the world. Save time on travel, record lectures and homework in your digital office.

Groups by age

We form groups taking into account age: children's (from 5 years old) and adult amateur groups. The program is adapted to the characteristics of information perception at different ages.

Questions about tournaments

Everything parents and young athletes need to know about qualifying competitions.

How does the game work in tournaments?
Games are played according to the Swiss system. The tournament is held without elimination. Starting from the second round, opponents are selected automatically so that participants who have scored an equal number of points meet each other. This guarantees a fair fight.
If a child loses on the first day, does he need to come back on the second day?
Yes, definitely! The participant has the right to play the tournament to the end regardless of the number of points scored or lost. Every round played is an experience that builds character and makes a child stronger.
Can parents enter the game room?
The presence of parents in the gaming room is strictly prohibited (except in force majeure situations by decision of the chief arbiter). This is necessary to maintain the concentration of players and eliminate hints. Parents can comfortably wait for their children in a specially equipped waiting area.
Where can I see the results of the played rounds?
All results and draws are promptly posted on the international websitechess-results.com. You need to go to the tournament search section, select the country KAZ (Kazakhstan) and find the name of the corresponding club cup.
How often do qualifying tournaments take place?
Qualifying tournaments for the assignment of 4, 3, 2 and 1 sports categories are held in our club monthly. A detailed schedule, regulations and links to registration are always published in advance on our official Instagram account.
